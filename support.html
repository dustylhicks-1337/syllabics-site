Syllabics Support

For help with a puzzle, saved progress, sound, or a technical issue, email us. Include your device model and iOS version, plus a short description of what happened. Please don’t include passwords or payment details.

syllabicswordfusion@gmail.com

Quick troubleshooting

  • If audio is quiet or muted, check the in-game music setting and your device’s silent or volume controls.
  • If a puzzle appears stuck, close and reopen the app. In your message, tell us the world and level so we can investigate.
  • Your progress and preferences are stored on your device. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting saved progress.
